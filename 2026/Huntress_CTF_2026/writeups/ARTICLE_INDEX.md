## Reading the labels

- **Investigation record:** an extended account containing an analysis sequence, examples, commands, or detailed evidence. This does not imply that all original artifacts or execution logs survive.
- **Session note:** a compact technical account of method and recorded outcome.
- **Evidence summary:** a bounded summary reconstructed from saved state, prompts, and tooling descriptions, without a complete command-by-command narrative.
- **Orientation note:** preserved onboarding material or an explicitly recorded gap.

Earlier and later accounts are labeled where both survive. First-person actions within those accounts belong to the recorded session; see the authorship note. Commands and `work/...` paths quoted from those sessions may use the original Homebase workspace layout. The tools guide describes the packaged layout.
