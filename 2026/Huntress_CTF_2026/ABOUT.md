# Authorship and evidence

This collection records work from Huntress CTF 2026: AI Arena. AI performed substantial analysis, generated solver code, and wrote the original session accounts. My role included directing the work, choosing and improving prompts, moving between tools, managing attempts and credits, and retaining the material. These articles should not be read as a claim that every technical action was performed manually.

The retained material comes from an early Codex workspace, later Homebase/Game AI work, a Codex consolidation, final prompt snapshots, a scoreboard export, and a completion certificate. The later SHA1-Hulud account also incorporates a separately retained ChatGPT web Pro-session writeup. First-person passages preserved inside a session account describe that account's actions.

## Editorial approach

The publication pass compared the sources, repaired clear assembly errors, separated historical attempts from final results, retained evidence limitations, and organized the material for GitHub. It did not rerun the CTF, submit answers, or recreate the unavailable event services.

An article's evidence label describes its documentation depth. It is not a new judgment about whether the platform awarded its points. Final completion claims use the saved scoreboard, while the certificate supplies the official final placement and participant count.

| Evidence | What it supports | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Completion certificate | Final place, points, and the platform's completion count | A complete set of individual solve logs |
| Saved scoring events | Recorded awards and their UTC timestamps | Elapsed solving time, a fully retained method, or a ranked Hall of Fame achievement |
| Retained notes and scripts | The documented analysis and available implementations | That every capability in a solver ran in every historical attempt |
| Narrative reports of earlier verification | What that earlier session reported | A fresh independent rerun in this publication pass |
| Reconstructed daily placement | Placement under the documented ordering and UTC cutoffs, using the retained entries | Official daily leaderboard captures or unobserved intraday peak ranks |

## Known boundaries

Some original artifacts, Homebase attempt directories, conversations, and raw acceptance responses were not retained. The SHA1-Hulud successful analysis is documented, but its complete original dynamic harness and reported companion bundle are not included. Earlier failed hypotheses remain identifiable as historical notes.

The final category prompts are snapshots, not a complete prompt version history. Their fixed assignments and any inconsistent release dates are explained alongside the originals; a reusable adaptation is kept separate.

Source snapshots may contain historical paths, usernames used by the challenge environment, mock credentials from challenge scenarios, and commands for event-only services. Connection setup and a saved pairing credential were excluded from the publication package. No original Git history is included. These exclusions do not imply that all challenge evidence can be reconstructed from the public files.

## Scope of validation

The publication package was checked for local Markdown links, JSON validity, Python syntax, accidental credential patterns, duplicate or misfiled articles, and consistency of its documented results. Placement was recomputed from the retained scoring histories. Python syntax checks do not establish solver correctness or compatibility with unavailable live services. See [results methodology](results/README.md) and the package's publishing review for the exact checks and remaining gaps.
